## About Me

I am a fourth-year Electrical and Computer Engineering student at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/){:target="\_blank"}. Under the guidance of Professor [Taskin Padir](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/padir-taskin/){:target="\_blank"} at [RIVeR Lab](https://robot.neu.edu/people/){:target="\_blank"}, I research robot manipulation. 

I am fortunate to be researching warehouse manipulation at [Amazon](https://www.amazon.science/research-areas/robotics){:target="\_blank"}.

In the past, I gained valuable industry research experience as a Research Intern at [OMRON SINIC X](https://www.omron.com/sinicx/){:target="\_blank"} in Tokyo where I researched contact-rich manipulation for soft robotics. I am also excited about open source, and previously received mentorship from the [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/){:target="\_blank"} while contributing to [SONiC](https://github.com/sonic-net/SONiC){:target="\_blank"}. I also worked with Professors [Alireza Ramezani](https://siliconsynapse.sites.northeastern.edu/){:target="\_blank"} and [Max Shepherd](https://shepherdlab.sites.northeastern.edu/){:target="\_blank"}.
